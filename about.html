About Us

Welcome to Punjab Rozgar, the leading platform for finding the latest government jobs in Punjab and across Pakistan. Our mission is to bridge the gap between job seekers and public sector organizations by providing timely, accurate, and easily accessible job updates.

We aggregate job listings from major testing agencies and official sources including PPSC, FPSC, NTS, and OTS. Our platform is completely free for all users.

If you have any questions or feedback, please feel free to contact us via WhatsApp.
